      Your data & setup

      Everything is processed in this browser. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.

      1. PG&E usage (Green Button)

      1. Sign in at pge.com and open Energy Usage Details.
      2. Click the green Green Button: Download my data button.
      3. Choose Export usage for a range of days, pick the last 12 months, format CSV.
      4. Drop the electric CSV here (you can ignore the gas file).
      No file loaded.

      2. Solar production

      1. Enphase: Enlighten app or web → Reports → Production → download CSV (hourly or 15-min).
      2. SolarEdge: monitoring.solaredge.com → Dashboard → Energy → Export to CSV.
      3. Tesla: Tesla app → Energy → Download My Data.
      4. No file? Leave this empty and the app estimates production from your system size.

      Why there's no “Sign in with PG&E” button

      PG&E's automatic data feed (Share My Data) only connects to registered companies that have gone through PG&E's third-party approval, not to a personal app. The Green Button download above gives the same hourly data. Re-download it every month or two to keep the app current. If you want it fully automatic, paid services such as UtilityAPI are registered with PG&E and can export your data on a schedule.
